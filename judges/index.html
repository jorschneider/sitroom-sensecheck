What we're doing here

We got 34 entries; 27 survived triage. The point of this contest is less to crown winners than to find people worth investing in and get them working on evals for strategic decision-making — a Signal group, feedback, a small honorarium, and a nudge to keep going. So please grade on a curve: an undergrad with a rough but real idea and a senior researcher with a polished benchmark are both interesting, for different reasons. Each card is labelled with career stage and technical vs policy background to help with that.

For each entry, pick one of three: Top-5 potential (the other judges should look and give notes), Encourage (deserves written feedback, a $250 honorarium, and a Signal-chat invite — tick the box if you'd also like the other judges to weigh in), or Pass (we shouldn't pull this person further into the project). Then write a few lines of feedback for the entrant — we'll send it to them, so write it to them. Everything saves in your browser as you go; hit the button at the bottom when you're done.
